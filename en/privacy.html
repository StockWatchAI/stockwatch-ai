Privacy Policy

StockWatch AI (provided by StockWatch Labs)

1. Introduction

StockWatch Labs ("we", "us") respects the privacy of everyone who uses the StockWatch AI app ("the app") and is committed to protecting personal information. This policy explains how information is handled in the app.

2. Information We Handle

2.1 Information you provide

  • The stocks on your watchlist (ticker symbols)
  • Your AI personality and summary style settings
  • Your app language and AI output language settings
  • Your custom prompt (free-text instructions for how summaries are written)

2.2 Information collected automatically

The app does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address or phone number. It does collect the following automatically:

  • An anonymous user ID (an identifier issued automatically by Firebase Authentication's anonymous sign-in; it does not identify you personally)
  • A device token for push notifications (FCM token)

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we handle only for the following purposes:

  • Retrieving and displaying share prices and news
  • Generating AI summaries
  • Storing your watchlist and settings
  • Delivering push notifications
  • Improving the app

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the third-party services listed below. Please also review each service's own privacy policy.

  • Yahoo Finance (share prices, price history, dividends, index levels and extended-hours quotes for all supported markets; symbol search): Yahoo Privacy Policy
  • Google News (news articles): Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI (generating AI summaries): OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Google) (anonymous sign-in, storing your watchlist and settings, delivering push notifications, and reading content we prepare): Google Privacy Policy

When an AI summary is generated, the price figures and the headlines and summary text of the news articles are sent to the OpenAI API. The ticker symbol you are viewing is sent to the price sources. The news sources receive the ticker symbol along with the company name and the language being searched. No information that directly identifies you is sent to any of these services.

Services the app no longer contacts directly

From version 2.0, news, earnings dates, company logos and extended-hours quotes for US stocks are fetched by our server and stored in Firebase; the app only reads them. Your device no longer communicates directly with:

  • Finnhub (news, earnings dates and company logos for US stocks) — fetched only by our server
  • Financial Modeling Prep — no longer used

These services can no longer see which stocks you are viewing. Our server fetches data using the combined list of stocks followed by all users, and does not send information about who follows which stock.

Filings that US companies submit to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) are retrieved from the SEC's EDGAR system by our server, summarized, and stored in Firebase. This happens on a schedule, independently of anything you do in the app, so the SEC does not receive information about your activity.

5. Data Storage

Your watchlist and AI settings are stored on your device (in UserDefaults).

In addition, to the extent needed to deliver push notifications, the following is stored in Firebase (Cloud Firestore) against your anonymous user ID:

  • The ticker symbols on your watchlist
  • Your device token for push notifications (FCM token)
  • Your notification preferences (which notifications you have chosen to receive)
  • Your app language and AI output language (used to decide which language notifications are written in)

Nothing beyond the above is stored. It contains no name, email address or other information that directly identifies you.

6. Deleting Your Data

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

The information held in Firebase (your anonymous user ID, ticker symbols, FCM token, notification preferences and language settings) remains on the server after the app is deleted. If you would like it removed, please contact us using the details in section 9. We will delete it within a reasonable period of your request.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as needed. If a change is significant, we will announce it in the app or on this page.
